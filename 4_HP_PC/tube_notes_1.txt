========OpenClaw======
- Software we install in a computer we own just not in our main computer and 
runs in the background
- Connects chat applications(Telegram, GPT) to our LLMs.(Gateway)
- Can run on anything---> raspberry
- Connect to the tools.
- Manage permissions---> Critical even though limits autonomy

=====OpenClaw Skills=====
- Ways we can provide extrafunctionality for our AI assistant
	`clawHub`
- The AI assistant(OpenClaw) can go to the clawHub find the skill and install it
- If cant find the skill write it and run.
- OpenClaw starts to evolve and becomes more useful the more you use it.
NOTE: * Can be compromised.
